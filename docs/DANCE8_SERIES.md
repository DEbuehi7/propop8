# Dance8 music-video series (proposal)

A plan for the next six music videos, from ChatGPT's strategy "AIM × Dance8:
The Next Music-Video Series" (13 Nov 2026), checked against canon 1.13.0 and
the reaction layer (D8-25, SPEC). It is a proposal: no canon entry is added or
changed, and every film below is a production brief, not a finished video or a
release date. The owner decides the open points at the end.

## The idea

One connected AIM music-video family instead of unrelated AI videos. Each
album keeps its own story, performers and look, and every film shares the same
G.A.T.E.S. grammar (Genome, AIM, Triadic, EDO, System; TRI-13), the Kirtled
move table (D8-20) and one Dance8 pipeline. A film should read as AIM because
its music, performers, wardrobe, instrument, movement, pattern, set and editing
follow one system, not because a logo is on screen.

The strategy works in four series stages: Signal (song, lyrics, voice,
harmony, rhythm), Form (EDO.PATTERN, triadic geometry, materials, wardrobe),
Movement (Kirtled choreography, gesture, skate-break, editing) and World (sets,
media walls, cinematic context). These are production stages around the
canonical DANCE8.TRIAD of Signal, Movement and Environment (vocab
TRIADIC_MEANINGS), not a new triad, and they are never labelled "Triadic".

Decision call: **EXECUTE** a small, reusable production system. **DEFER** any
physical building or instrument-control claim until its evidence exists.

## What every film keeps

- **Instrument.** The Y of three equal arms at 120 degrees, eight triads per
  arm, with the arm contents of D8-21. It is a specified, conceptual instrument
  (TRI-06 CONCEPTUAL), not a manufactured one. No new control layouts between
  clips.
- **Moves.** The six-move table of D8-20. Skaters land one trick per chord
  change (D8-16); the owner's filmed dance is read only on the beat grid
  (D8-17, D8-18).
- **Performers.** The trio are fictional roles in teal, magenta and gold, with
  voices only from the approved voice map (D8-22). Uploaded master images and
  approved character designs are the reference; concept images never replace
  them.
- **Wardrobe.** Proposed shared look: matte-black outer layer, restrained gold
  trim, geometry built into the panels, a small identity accent per performer.
  Seamless Threads itself is research (WAR-01): on screen it is costume, with
  no claim that a garment senses, heals, heats or cools.
- **Pattern.** EDO.PATTERN motifs keep their Edo and Benin attribution
  (GOV-21); genome graphics are design grammar, not biology (TRI-13). A pattern
  appears for a reason, tied to a measured or verified music event.
- **Timing.** Events sit on the checked audio timeline (D8-01), clocks never
  mix (D8-02), and big motion stays slower than the beat (D8-03).
- **Labels.** Titles, credits and captions are a separate approved layer,
  never baked into generated images (GOV-23).

## Music events and the reaction layer

The strategy's cause-and-effect table, against what D8-25 does today
([DANCE8_REACTION_LAYER.md](DANCE8_REACTION_LAYER.md)). The three families
become reusable presets, rendered from Stuttr's saved feature track, never
written into Kling prompts or regenerated at random.

| Music event | Strategy | D8-25 today | To get there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vocal stutter or onset | Genome Loom thread pulse or controlled replay | Onset-like peaks travel as Loom knots (MEASURED, light only); a verified lyric chop adds a magenta knot | A stutter is drawn only as a verified lyric chop. Any "replay" is a picture edit; the audio is never touched |
| Harmony change | Kirtled chord-state display; a dance cue after validation | A verified chord change adds a Cypher landing ring; held otherwise | A verified chord chart per song. A Tonnetz chord display is a D8-10 diagram, not part of the reaction layer |
| Dance movement | Cypher floor path and pose-state overlay | Move words go to the caption file only, never the picture | Drawing verified move words is allowed by D8-25 but not built; a pose overlay needs pose data that does not exist |
| Frequency-band change | Resonance Chamber | Low, mid and high bands drive size, spikes and ripples | Built |
| Silence or DASH | Visual hold, reduced motion, sustained light | Low energy dims the families; nothing holds on a freeze | A verified freeze may hold the overlay. The reduced-motion version stays an accessibility setting, not a musical effect |

One validated event track can drive every album's look, so the music is
analysed once per song, not re-edited per style.

## Pipeline

Simple outside, rigorous inside, with the tools that exist (no new video app):

1. **Suno and Stuttr:** approved master, vocals, stems and rights record
   (STU-01, STU-02).
2. **Stuttr analysis and Dance8 timing:** measured timing, verified harmony,
   Kirtled move words, scene events.
3. **Visual library:** approved performers, sets, materials, album wall packs
   and effect presets.
4. **Kling:** selective new shots only.
5. **Reaction layer:** timing-accurate light, patterns and diagrams (D8-25).
6. **Dance8 assembly, review and owner approval:** render, sync QA, sign-off.

Tool facts the strategy relied on, checked 13 Nov 2026:

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Suno Studio delivers all audio exports as WAV; its Multitrack option exports every track as stems. The article does not say which plans include it | [Suno Help, Exporting from Studio](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13925249), edited 9 Sep 2026 |
| Kling 4.0 takes up to 10 keyframe images and up to 15 combined reference assets (images, videos, subjects, voices) per task, and generates 3 to 30 seconds natively | [Kling, Multiple Keyframes guide](https://kling.ai/blog/kling-4-multiple-keyframes-guide), 30 Sep 2026 |

Kling's references help keep performers and sets consistent; they never
replace Dance8's audio-to-frame plan. The strategy adds a CapCut review step;
CapCut is not in the vendor register (ledger 11).

## The virtual facility

A recurring fictional set, so each album has a recognisable AIM home without
every song happening in one room. Three connected spaces:

| Space | Used for | Reusable assets |
|---|---|---|
| Signal Lab | Singing, recording, Kirtled harmony | Microphone views, instrument close-ups, vocal-reactive walls |
| Movement Hall | Dance, trio formations, floorwork, skate-break | Triadic floor, camera paths, rehearsal presets |
| Environment Studio | World changes, album walls, post-production imagery | Genome Loom, SontheraVein worlds, TIME AND SOUND effects |

The hall joins the three, and their roles are not read off the instrument's
three arms. Rules for the set:

- It is fiction, built only from Dance8 and Stuttr assets. No real parcel,
  address, street, signage or interior, and no likeness of an employer's
  building (GOV-16).
- It never draws on AIM-B5R work. Skid Row SRO is an AIM-B5R research market
  (B5R-12), and media and property data never merge (GOV-15).
- A physical version stays a concept: no lot is selected, so it is GOV-30
  HOLD, and a live-work and performance building is mixed use, which never
  enters underwriting (B5R-33).
- Its name is the owner's call (ledger 1).

## Release checks and measures

| Check | Evidence | Releases when |
|---|---|---|
| Source audio | Approved master, its hash, a dated rights record | Verified |
| Timing | Frames computed from the recording | No unreviewed drift |
| Performer identity | Approved images and voice permissions | Stable across shots |
| Kirtled moves | D8-20 and verified audio events | No invented move-to-sound claim |
| Instrument | D8-21 | Stable across images and video |
| Album walls | Album, track and scene reference | No unrelated content |
| Reaction layer | D8-25 evidence and motion-safety checks | Verified behaviour |
| Production value | Editing time, cost, rejected clips, reused assets | Measured against a baseline |

Track per film: cost per approved second, sync errors, clip rejection rate,
reuse of approved assets and time to finish. No improvement percentage is
claimed before it is measured (D8-05).

Baseline: EDO V3 is 3,022 frames at 24 fps, 125.92 seconds (2:05.92), as
rendered on 11 Nov 2026.

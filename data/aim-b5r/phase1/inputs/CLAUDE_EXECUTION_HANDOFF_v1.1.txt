Phase 1 field contract per property

Immutable UUID; original lead address; county; official situs address; APN(s) and formatted variants; active parcel status; APN history (NOT_AVAILABLE unless evidenced); incorporated status and municipality; planning and zoning authorities; geometry match, GIS layer identifier/version and limitations; assessor source URL and retrieval timestamp; zoning/jurisdiction source URL and timestamp; conflicts; title/recorder retrieval status; human reviewer; evidence snapshot ID; next action; gate.
For every material fact preserve source URI, source publisher, observed/effective date (if supplied), retrieval timestamp, geographic coverage, value, state (verified/provisional/conflicting/missing/stale), and reason for uncertainty. Do not label a public map a surveyed boundary.

Gates

PASS: official APN/address match, competent jurisdiction confirmed, screening-level parcel geometry reconciled; source and date preserved.
CONDITIONAL: core identity verified; explicitly noncritical gaps remain.
HOLD: APN, jurisdiction or geometry materially unresolved; investigate or request human access.
FAIL: documented material identity contradiction or confirmed ineligibility; include evidence and reviewer.
Missing evidence is not equivalent to a negative fact. No auto-BUY. Human approval for binding acquisition decisions.

Implementation constraints

Add minimal adapters and evidence records; do not create new agents, elaborate twins, EDO pricing models, or Stuttr functionality. Preserve legal/physical/advertised units as separate facts.
